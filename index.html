    MISSION BRIEFING

    How to prepare and launch

    Keep the crew alive for a randomized mission of 4–14 sols. Mission duration, resource budgets, and event odds refresh for every new mission.

    1. Review the forecast. Click MISSION PLAN on the left edge, then click MISSION WEATHER FORECAST to expand it. Each row shows a Sol, event likelihood, and recommended countermeasure. Each event rolls from 0–99 and occurs only when the roll is below its displayed percentage.
    2. Complete launch objectives. Deploy the two systems listed under MISSION OBJECTIVES and marked REQUIRED. Objectives change each mission; follow any REQUIRES tags to deploy prerequisites. The checklist updates as you deploy, and ADVANCE MISSION remains locked until both objectives are complete. Before launch, click REMOVE to change your plan; removing a prerequisite also removes its dependent systems. Balance the mass and power meters, and prioritize countermeasures for forecast events.
    3. Launch the mission. Once both current objectives are complete, click ADVANCE MISSION. Flight Data opens on the right, and both side menus can stay open together.
    4. Manage each Sol. Click ADVANCE SOL to continue. Watch crew health, oxygen, food, habitat integrity, surface conditions, and the flight log. Events are rolled against their displayed likelihoods.
    5. Develop your program. Each completed mission grants 10 XP. Level requirements rise by 100 XP per level through Level 10; every level-up awards an upgrade point. Open UPGRADES at the bottom right of the 3D display to spend points on power capacity, oxygen recovery, food production, or crew medical output.
